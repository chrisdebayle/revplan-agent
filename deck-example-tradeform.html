What could break this, and what I am doing about it
Risk 01
Burning the list before it’s warm

Enrich and verify numbers first so calls hit real people, not office lines, before ramping.

Risk 02
Wrong tool kills connections

Use a sequential dialer, not parallel, to avoid the connect latency that loses the person.

Risk 03
Meetings booked, then dropped

A qualified meeting with no owner goes cold. Each one is handed to the TradeForm team with full context, not just a calendar invite.
