04

The Rail

The forecast gate for every deal in this territory. A deal doesn't count as real until it clears all five wins.
What each win requires
V
Validated

The technical evaluation is won on criteria the buyer's own team set, not on criteria we assumed for them.

A
Aligned

A champion inside the account is actively selling this internally, and more than one person there is engaged.

L
Locked

A named sponsor has committed to a decision date, and there's a real consequence if that date slips.

I
Invested

Budget is confirmed directly from the person who owns it, not inferred from a hopeful conversation.

D
Documented

Legal and procurement have a clear, redlined path to signature.
